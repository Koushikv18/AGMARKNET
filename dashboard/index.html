Seasonal Pattern

Kharif / Rabi Price Cycles

Prices typically fall at harvest (Oct–Jan for Kharif, Mar–May for Rabi) and spike in lean months. The trend line exposes whether this cycle is amplifying over years.

Inflation Signal

Multi-Year Price Drift

Plotting 2013–2025 reveals the compounded inflation in agricultural commodities — critical context for MSP adequacy analysis.

Drought Impact

Shock Detection

Sudden price spikes (>2σ from rolling mean) in specific months correspond to drought years, floods, or supply chain disruptions (e.g. COVID-19, 2020).

High Impact

Vegetables & Perishables

Onion, tomato, and potato typically appear in both the top-volume list and the highest-volatility list — supply shocks translate directly into consumer price inflation.

Policy Lever

MSP Commodities

Rice, wheat, and major pulses (tur/dal, moong) are MSP-supported. Tracking whether their modal price stays above MSP is a direct policy audit metric.

Stable

Spices & Cash Crops

Turmeric, chilli, and cotton typically show lower relative volatility but much higher absolute prices — important for export income tracking.

Market Inefficiency — Price Spread
Exploitation Proxy
High spread % (max−min / modal) indicates wider intra-day price dispersion — a proxy for opaque, inefficient markets where farmers may be underpaid.
Arbitrage Signal — State vs National Avg
Price Gouging Detection
Red cells = states charging significantly above the national average for the same commodity on the same day. Persistent red = structural price exploitation.
MSP Violation

Below-MSP Price Detection

When modal_price falls below the declared MSP for a commodity, it signals either data quality issues or genuine MSP violations — both actionable for policy enforcement. This pipeline can be extended to flag these rows directly.

Price Gouging

Cross-State Arbitrage

When the same commodity (same date, same grade) trades at >30% above the national average in a specific state, it suggests supply bottlenecks, cartelisation, or poor inter-state market integration — all targets for APMC reform.

Farmer Welfare

Income Risk Index

High price volatility (CV%) directly translates into income uncertainty for farmers. Commodities with CV > 40% are candidates for derivative instruments or government price stabilisation schemes.

Local Mode

No Cluster Required

Set USE_HDFS=false and SPARK_MASTER=local[*] in .env to run the entire pipeline on a laptop. PySpark will use all CPU cores.

YARN / Cluster

Scale Out

Set SPARK_MASTER=yarn, USE_HDFS=true, and HDFS_NAMENODE=hdfs://<host>:9000 for a full cluster run. The HDFS ingestion script handles the upload.

API Rate Limits

Full Pull Strategy

With the demo API key, a full 2013–2025 pull may take 24+ hours due to rate limiting. Register a free key at data.gov.in for ~10× higher limits. The script resumes automatically on restart.
